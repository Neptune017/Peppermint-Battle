     QUICK GUIDE

    How to play

    Build your field, protect your life, and break through the rival’s lanes.

    01 · PLAY A FIGHTERChoose a card in your hand, then place it in an open lane. Pay its energy cost. Newly summoned cards cannot attack during their first turn. Action cards have no element and cannot be summoned.
    02 · ATTACKAttacks are manual. Most fighters attack the lane directly in front. Shooters can attack a Fighter in either adjacent lane or the Life Zone opposite them directly.
    03 · BREAK LIFEAttack the Life Zone in front of your fighter. When all three middle Life Zones are empty, the Main Life can be attacked from any lane with no opposing fighter in that lane. A Shooter in the middle lane can attack Main Life past the opposing center fighter.
    04 · BUILD ENERGYEnergy refills each turn and its maximum grows by one. Draw one card each turn.
    05 · HAND LIMITYour starting hand contains 5 cards. You can hold up to 7 cards. If you have more than 7, immediately choose cards to send to your Pandemonium Zone until you have 7.
    Familiar: A Summoner can place a same-element Creature in its empty lane Life Zone as a Familiar. The Familiar gives its Fighter +1 attack while both remain on the field, and is destroyed if its owner is defeated.
    Battle rule: Only the Life Zone your attack is allowed to reach is damaged. Shooter attacks can bypass fighters to reach the Life Zone directly opposite them.

     ELEMENT MATCHUPS

    Element advantage

    A Fighter gains +1 attack when it attacks a Fighter whose element it beats. Shooters compare elements with the Fighter in the lane they target. Attacks on Life Zones do not receive this bonus.
